Cookie Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026.

Cookies are small files stored in your browser. Below we explain which technologies this website uses.

1. What we store

This website does not use its own cookies, analytics or advertising tools.

The only thing we store in your browser (localStorage) is whether you agreed to display the Google map. It is needed so we do not ask you on every visit. You can delete it at any time (see below).

2. Google Maps

We load the Google map showing the Salon’s location only after you click “Load map”. After you consent, Google may store its own cookies in your browser and process your IP address, as described in Google’s privacy policy. Until you consent, no data is sent to Google for this purpose.

Instead of the map you can use the “Open in Google Maps” link.

3. Google Fonts

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts servers. This does not store cookies, but Google receives your IP address. See our Privacy Policy for more.

4. Links to other services

The website contains links to Booksy, Instagram, WhatsApp and Telegram. Once you go to those services, their own cookie rules apply.

5. How to manage consent and cookies

You can withdraw consent for the map with the button below. You can also delete or block cookies and site data at any time in your browser settings.

6. Contact

If you have questions, contact us by phone (+48 577 418 424) or on Instagram @ocean.beauty.wola. More about data protection in our Privacy Policy.
